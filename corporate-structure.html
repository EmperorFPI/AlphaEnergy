Corporate Structure

Group structure

Alpha Energy is the parent. Alpha Latin America (SPV) and Alpha Ukraine are wholly owned subsidiaries. U.S. assets are held on the U.S. side of the group, including a 50% interest in TPIC held through Alpha Parker Creek.

Notes on the structure

U.S. producing assets are held directly by Alpha Energy as the approved operator. The interest in Texas Petroleum Investment Company (TPIC) is a 50% holding through Alpha Parker Creek — an associate interest, not a wholly owned subsidiary. International operations are ring-fenced within dedicated wholly owned subsidiaries to align ownership, financing and regulatory treatment with each jurisdiction.
