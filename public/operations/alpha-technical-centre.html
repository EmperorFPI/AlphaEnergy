Capability

One integrated technical workflow

The Alpha Technical Centre exists to convert technical potential into recoverable barrels and resilient cash flow. It brings the disciplines that usually sit in silos — geoscience, drilling, completions, production and finance — into one multidisciplinary team working a single decision loop.

/ 01

Subsurface & Reservoir

Static and dynamic modelling, reserves and resource assessment, well spacing and full-field development planning.

/ 02

Completions & Diagnostics

Completion design, fracture modelling, diagnostic program design and interpretation for brownfield optimisation.

/ 03

Production & EOR

Workover and recompletion programs, artificial-lift optimisation, waterflood and enhanced-recovery design.

/ 04

Financial Engineering

Full-cycle economic modelling, project-finance structuring and risk analysis linking the subsurface to the balance sheet.

/ 05

Data & Analytics

Advanced analytics applied to large datasets — custom visualisation and multivariate workflows that drive better decisions.

/ 06

Field Development & Execution

Infrastructure rehabilitation, flowline and facilities scope, and phased mobilisation of brownfield redevelopment.
